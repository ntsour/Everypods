# Peer warning terminology

The Home warning describes the EveryPods peer connection, independently of the
AirPods audio connection. The English heading is **Peer disconnected**.
The technical nouns below are attested in networking documentation or academic
literature. The complete warning sentences are UI translations, not quotations
from those sources. Multiple translations of “peer” exist; these are the selected
terms for this warning, not claims of a single universal standard.

Singular and multiple-peer headings use the same terminology. Counted headings
use a label followed by the count where that avoids number-dependent noun forms.
Headings may wrap rather than truncate at large font sizes. Expanded text retains
the peer name and explains the impact on handover and case-open connection.
