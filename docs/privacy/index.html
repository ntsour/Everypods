EveryPods Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

EveryPods is an Android app for compatible AirPods controls. It is designed to keep its settings and connection state on your device. This policy explains the data the app handles and the limited circumstances in which data leaves your device.

Data handled on your device

EveryPods stores Bluetooth device identifiers and connection state, feature preferences, and local diagnostic information on your Android device so its features can work. If you configure it, the app also stores an optional ElevenLabs API key and text-to-speech preference locally.

EveryPods does not include advertising or analytics SDKs, and it does not sell your personal data.

Optional cloud text-to-speech

EveryPods includes an optional ElevenLabs text-to-speech engine for notification and battery announcements. This feature is off unless you select ElevenLabs and enter an API key that you control.

When enabled, the announcement text, selected voice and language settings, and your API key are transmitted directly to ElevenLabs over HTTPS so it can generate speech. EveryPods does not operate a proxy and does not receive a copy of that information. ElevenLabs processes that data under its own terms and privacy policy.

If you do not enable ElevenLabs, announcements use Android system text-to-speech and are not sent to ElevenLabs. You can remove an API key at any time in the app settings.

Permissions

EveryPods asks Android for permissions only to provide the features you choose to use. These can include Bluetooth discovery and connection, notifications, foreground connected-device operation, optional call controls, notification announcements, accessibility-based camera controls, overlays, startup after a restart, and battery-optimization exclusions. Android shows and manages these permissions.

Your choices and data deletion

You can change EveryPods settings, revoke permissions, remove an optional API key, or stop using a feature at any time. You can delete locally stored app data by clearing EveryPods storage in Android Settings or by uninstalling the app.

Support and external links

When you choose a support or repository link, EveryPods opens your selected email or browser app. Information you send through those services is handled by the relevant provider. Do not post Bluetooth addresses, API keys, or diagnostic logs containing personal information in public issue trackers.
